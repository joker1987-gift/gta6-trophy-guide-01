    About this guide. Trophy names, descriptions and grades are the official PlayStation list for Grand Theft Auto VI (55 trophies: 1 platinum, 2 gold, 6 silver, 46 bronze; 28 hidden; no online trophies), as published by Exophase and PSNProfiles, which agree on every entry. Every guide here is written for this app and cites its sources beside the trophy; where a trigger is not yet confirmed by a source we trust, the guide says so instead of inventing steps.
